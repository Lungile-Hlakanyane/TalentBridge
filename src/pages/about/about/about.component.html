Our Story

Founded in 2025 by a team of ex-recruiters and engineers, TalentBridge started after we saw how broken hiring was on both sides. Candidates felt invisible. Companies wasted months on bad fits. We knew tech + empathy could solve it. Today, we’re the fastest growing recruitment platform in Bloemfontein, Free State, with plans to expand across SA.
